I/O

The software Crown Roots Foundation builds and owns to close the information gap for Pocono Mountain families.

Two Tools, One Path

Families here are not short on talent or effort. They are short on information: what college really costs, which schools meet full need, and what steps to take in which year. I/O is how we put that information in their hands.

In is what a family learns. Out is where a student goes.

In/Root, n. a family's insight into The Gap, school by school; a student en route to their own growth.
Out/Come, n. a Crown Roots student's path out into the world, and their path back into the community they come from.
Free and open to everyone

In/Root

College Search Database

Search colleges by what a family at your income level would actually pay, not the sticker price. Built so any Pocono family can see the real numbers before they rule a school out.
