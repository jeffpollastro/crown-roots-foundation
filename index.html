Problem

Pocono Mountain has the scholars, the athletes, and the future leaders. What it doesn't have is access: to the information that shows a family its real college price, to mentorship, and to the campuses that show kids how big the world really is. Families here make decisions about college and careers from what they can see locally, not from everything that is open to them.

Crown Roots Foundation exists to change that through three programs. Each one closes a different part of the gap, and each one depends on funding to reach more families.

Academics and Campus Visits

Our direct-service program. We help students build a strong academic record, work through college applications and financial aid with their families, and see campuses in person.

Provide

Program Access

Campus visits organized from start to finish, so students can see a college for themselves. Right now families pay a $75 trip fee that covers transportation, and we’re pursuing grants to help eliminate that cost, family by family.

See our Youngstown State trip →

Access to Information

College guidance, the real price of college for each family, and help with applications, the FAFSA, and scholarships. On campus visits, students see where they’ll actually spend their time, not just the tour route.

See our tools →

Time Help

Streamlining coaching resources, admin best practices, and success pathways so organizations can focus on what matters: the kids. Coaches clinics, shared support, and spotlights of success.
