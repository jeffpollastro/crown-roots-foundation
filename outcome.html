You Were Invited for a Reason

Out/Come is not open to the public, and that is on purpose. Every account starts with a conversation. A Crown Roots staff member sits down with a student and their family, listens, and builds a plan around that one student. What you find inside was put together for you.

Why It's Private

Out/Come holds real information about real students: grades, goals, and family circumstances. Only you and Crown Roots staff can see your account.

Have an Invitation?

Your Crown Roots advisor will give you a sign-up link. Create your account, and your advisor takes it from there. If you can't find your link, ask your advisor.

How Out/Come Works

For partners and supporters, here is what happens on the staff side.

Intake

A staff member walks through a structured intake with the student: grades, interests, household situation, and goals. Nothing is filled out alone.

Pathway and Progress

Each student gets a high school pathway and a progress tracker, so everyone can see what has been done and what comes next.

College and Scholarship Matches

The system matches each student to affordable colleges and scholarships they are eligible for, along with test prep dates and deadlines.

Staff Review

Our team reviews every match before a student sees it. Students do not browse raw data. They see a short list that has been checked and explained.

Not a Crown Roots Student?

In/Root is for everyone. It is our free college search, built so any family can see what college would really cost them before ruling a school out.
